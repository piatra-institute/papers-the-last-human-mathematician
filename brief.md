# Brief

## Question

The origin material proposes a title and a thesis: "computer" once named a person and now names a machine; 1976, the year of the four-colour theorem, can stand for that transfer; 2036 may stand for the same transfer of "mathematician". A working dossier in the same folder qualifies the thesis: it separates capability, autonomy, research production, professional jurisdiction and the default meaning of the noun, treats 2036 as a horizon, audits the 2026 evidence on machine mathematics, and proposes a corpus and vignette study of the word. Its own priority for a first empirical addition is a pilot of the semantic-default measurement. The questions are: what actually happened to the word "computer" and when, what does the 2026 record of machine mathematics establish, which parts of mathematical work and authority can move without the word, and what would count, in 2036, as the end of the presumption that a mathematician is a person?

## Claim

1. The first transfer can be dated from the books themselves. Writers marked the machine sense of "computer" with a qualifier ("electronic", "digital", "automatic") in 22.7% of uses at the 1955 peak, fewer than half that by 1966 and a tenth by 1980; by 1952 most printed uses referred to machines even if every wartime use is counted as human. Contrastive "human computer" peaked earlier, in 1950. The noun's default moved two decades before NASA's human computing ended, and by 1976 the machine qualifier had fallen to 14.7% of its peak.
2. A second transfer, "typewriter", resolved the other way: the operator received a new noun ("typist"), and person-marked forms peaked in 1898.
3. In October 2026 the word "mathematician" shows the opening of a contrastive phase without extension: "human mathematician" rose to 1.5% of scholarly works using the noun (11.5 times its 2016 to 2024 rate after deduplication), almost always beside AI terms, while machines are rarely called mathematicians (6 works in 2026). The marking pattern is the inverse of 1950, when the machine sense was marked 111 times as often as the human sense.
4. Five transitions (capability, operational autonomy, research production, jurisdiction, semantic default) can separate, and two outcomes (human understanding, human control) can move independently of them. The verified 2026 record supports machine research episodes on selected problems with formal checks and correction histories; it does not support a dated forecast of dominance.
5. Proof, understanding and value are distinct; jurisdiction and labor can shift without the word; open results do not imply open means of discovery.
6. A protocol with three separately preregistered claims for 2036 (research origin, professional authority, semantic default), with the corpus measurement of this paper as its baseline.

## Kind

Essay with a corpus analysis. has_simulation: true (the analysis code reads frozen corpus data); claims bound in claims.yaml to simulation/output/results.json and to checked sources.

## Discipline

No claim of a last use of either word; no forecast of a year. The 2026 technical claims are reported as their sources report them, with their correction histories; the mathematics in them was not checked here. The corpus numbers are properties of two named corpora at a stated retrieval date, frozen in the repository, with their known biases stated. The origin material's dates (2032 or 2036, milestone ranges) are treated as conjectures and not repeated as estimates.
